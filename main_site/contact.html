Join econai@kaist

We are looking for students and researchers who want to pursue rigorous, ambitious research with meaningful implications for society.

Background

We particularly welcome applicants with a background in economics, computer science/AI, or mathematics, along with strong quantitative skills. You do not need training in all of these fields—we value a willingness to learn and work across disciplines.

What we value

  • A growth mindset. You seek feedback, reflect on setbacks, and actively develop new skills and ways of thinking.
  • Initiative and ownership. You identify worthwhile questions and useful next steps, take responsibility for your work, and follow through.
  • Clear thinking and writing. You build logical arguments, examine evidence carefully, and communicate your ideas clearly in writing.
  • A collaborative spirit. You share ideas and work in progress, engage constructively with different perspectives, and contribute to your teammates’ progress.

Research requires sustained effort and persistence. Our projects sometimes move quickly, and we value people who can adapt, communicate proactively, and maintain care in their work as priorities evolve.

Get in touch

If our research and approach resonate with you, we would be glad to hear from you. Please email Professor Jihee Kim with a brief introduction, your research interests, and your CV. Tell us what questions you would like to explore and how your interests connect with our work.

For research collaborations and other inquiries, please contact us at the same address.
