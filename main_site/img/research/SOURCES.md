# Research figure sources

Research images retain their original scientific content and colors. Any panel selection or cropping is noted below.

- `hwanghae-crop-map-2025.png`: The 2025 panel of Figure 10 in the KAIST–Stanford 2026 Ministry of Unification final report. Lossless crop at x=1365, y=0, width=683, height=565 from the 2048×662 report image; map pixels and colors are unchanged. The original Korean legend is replaced by an accessible English HTML legend on the Research page. The figure shows estimated rice, maize, and other-crop distributions for selected cropland in South and North Hwanghae at 10 m resolution. Inputs: Sentinel-2; cropland mask: ESA WorldCover 2020 v100 and slope ≤10°; administrative boundaries: GADM 4.1.
