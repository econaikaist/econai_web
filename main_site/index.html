Our mission

At econai@kaist, we bring economics and artificial intelligence together to study economic and social change.

We investigate how AI understands and reasons about the world, and when it can reliably support economic and social research. We also explore how interactions among people and AI agents may reshape economic outcomes.

We use AI to measure economic change where conventional data fall short.
