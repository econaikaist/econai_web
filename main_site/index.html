Our mission

At econai@kaist, we bring economics and artificial intelligence together to understand economic and social change. Our work connects research on human decisions and collective behavior with questions about AI’s capabilities and role in society.

Understanding People, Markets, and AI

We see AI as both a new way to study the world and an increasingly important part of it. Using AI and computational methods, we investigate how people make decisions and how their interactions shape markets and societies. As AI systems enter these interactions, understanding how they reason and behave becomes essential—both to using them reliably in research and to understanding their economic and social consequences.

Measuring Economic Change with AI

We also use AI to expand what we can observe, drawing on satellite imagery and geospatial data to measure economic change where conventional data fall short, including in North Korea. By connecting better measurement with a deeper understanding of human and AI behavior, we aim to generate evidence that informs economic research and public decisions.
