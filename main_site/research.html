Research

Research Areas

We combine artificial intelligence, large-scale data, and empirical analysis to study economic and social systems.

01

Economic Measurement from Geospatial and Large-Scale Data

How can we measure overlooked places and populations when reliable ground truth is limited?

We develop machine learning methods that turn satellite imagery, spatial signals, and other large-scale data into reliable measures of local economic activity, inequality, environmental exposure, and social change.

Selected Publications

02

LLMs for Economic Reasoning and Research

What do language models know about causal evidence, and when can researchers trust their reasoning?

We build benchmarks and empirical evaluations that reveal how language models retrieve, represent, and reason over economic and scientific evidence, while exploring responsible ways to use them in research workflows.

Selected Publications

  • Ideological Bias in LLMs’ Economic Causal Reasoning Conference on Language Modeling (COLM 2026) Fig. 2, Lee et al. (2026)
  • In-Context Examples Suppress Scientific Knowledge Recall in LLMs Conference on Language Modeling (COLM 2026) Hidden-state comparison, Jang et al. (2026)
03

AI, Technology, and Social & Policy Outcomes

How do emerging technologies change decisions, institutions, organizations, and social outcomes?

We use computational and causal methods to study how AI and digital technologies interact with firms, public policy, human behavior, and collective decision-making.
